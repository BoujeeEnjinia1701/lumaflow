# LumaFlow user requirements

What users need, written from their side. Product requirements that meet these needs are in PRS (`03-requirements.md`, LMF-PRS-001) and the links are in the traceability matrix (`03b-traceability.md`, LMF-RTM-001). Method: STANDARDS section 22. The sources are the user and stakeholder tables of LMF-PRB-001 (Tables 1 and 2); these needs are proposals until they are reviewed with the co-design partner, a university extension programme for private well owners (LMF-DEC-001), which has not yet been approached.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Safer water at one tap | Water from my drinking tap has bacteria and protozoan cysts reduced to a level I can trust, in the clear, filtered well water I have | High | Well household (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Useful flow | The tap fills a 1 L bottle in under a minute | High | Well household; clinic (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-003 | Performance | Poorer water | If my water is cloudier than the design water, the unit still treats it or tells me that it cannot | Medium | Municipal household with events (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-004 | Safety | No silent failure | The unit never passes untreated water without stopping it and telling me | High | All households; clinic (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-005 | Usability | Instant on | Water is treated the moment I open the tap, with no warm-up and nothing to switch on | High | Well household (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-006 | Environment and durability | No mercury | Nothing in the unit can release mercury into my water or home | High | Well household (PRB problem statement) |
| UR-007 | Environment and durability | Household pressure | The unit copes with the pressure in my pipes, including surges, without leaking or breaking | High | Well household; installer (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-008 | Usability | Tap pressure | The unit does not noticeably weaken the flow at my tap | Medium | Well household (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-009 | Cost | Low running energy | It uses little electricity, and almost none when the tap is closed | Medium | Well household; off-grid users (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-010 | Environment and durability | Long life | It keeps working for years without parts wearing out from heat | Medium | Clinic with no technician (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-011 | Safety | Nothing added | Nothing in the unit adds harmful substances to the water | High | All users (PRB constraints) |
| UR-012 | Safety | No UV exposure | No one can be exposed to the UV-C light, even when opening the unit | High | All users; maker (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-013 | Safety | Electrical safety | It is safe to have in a wet cabinet next to water pipes | High | All users (PRB constraints) |
| UR-014 | Deployment and assembly | Fits under the sink | It fits under a kitchen sink beside the cold line | High | Well household; installer (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-015 | Maintenance | Easy service | I or a local helper can replace the parts that wear with hand tools, quickly | Medium | Clinic; maker (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-016 | Cost | Affordable and buildable | A maker can build it from catalogue parts at a cost a household could consider | Medium | Maker; household (PRB constraints) |
| UR-017 | Usability | Status at a glance | I can see at a glance that it is working and when it needs service | Medium | Clinic; household (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-018 | Regulatory | Honest claims | The unit and its documents say plainly what it is, a research and educational prototype, and never claim certified safe water | High | Certification bodies; all users (PRB Table 2 and constraints) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
