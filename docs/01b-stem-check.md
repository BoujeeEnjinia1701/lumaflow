# LumaFlow STEM check

Verdict: the working principle of option A, the existing design (a flow-switched UV-C LED reactor that shines along a reflective, water-filled tube and stops the water when its wall sensor reads too little light), sits within known science. Of the 51 rows, **39 are within known science and 12 are plausible, to confirm**. Four details of option A failed and were fixed in the same session under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23. The first run found two: UV-C refracted outward in the quartz window fell on the rubber (EPDM) window gasket and sensor window washer (S9; both are now expanded PTFE), and the retaining ring screws, at the torque the build plan gave, bent the 2 mm stainless ring to about its proof stress (S12; now anti-seize and 0.21 N·m). The independent review found two more: with the normally closed valve downstream of the flow sensor, opening the faucet could never start the unit (S37; an outlet pressure sensor now starts it), and the alarm threshold, set from an unweighted dose, let the true dose fall to about 37 mJ/cm² and missed a failed LED (S38; now set from the 275 nm weighted dose). Seven of option A's plausible rows need measurement or the chosen parts' datasheets, not calculation: photorepair (S6), regrowth while the water stands (S7), light through the PTFE onto the acetal cap (S10), the valve's flow coefficient (S22), LED derating (S33, worst case 37.5 mJ/cm²), mixing in the reactor (S34) and the photodiode's spectral response (S39). So `stem_check` stays **open**; the repo keeps its TRL 3 under STANDARDS section 20, and the dose margin question is with Amish (LMF-DEC-001, O-3); the mixing question was decided by Amish on 2026-10-25 (O-4 (a): keep the side inlet, tracer test at TRL 4). Method: STANDARDS section 20. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, which imports the calculation note's script `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (LMF-CAL-001), so the rows rerun whenever the model changes.

## Concept options

- **Option A, axial UV-C LED reactor:** S1 to S39. Within known science after the four fixes; plausible rows S6, S7, S10, S22, S33, S34 and S39 wait for datasheets or TRL 4 measurements.
- **Option B, hollow-fibre ultrafilter:** within known science in principle (size exclusion, B2; integrity test well below the bubble point, B4). Plausible: membrane permeability for 1.2 L/min at 0.5 bar (B1), virus removal (B3, not credited) and the housing pressure rating (B5). Not dropped.
- **Option C, radial UV-C LED reactor:** within known science for dose order of magnitude (C1), quartz tube stress (C3), cooling through the water (C4) and seals out of the light (C5); its RED (C2) and sensor mapping (C6) need a ray trace. It draws about 39 W from the mains while water flows, which misses PR-009 (a requirement, not a science failure; scored in LMF-OPT-001). Not dropped.
